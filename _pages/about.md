Born in the UK, I am an artist and scientist living and working in Innsbruck. My artistic work and research explores the boundaries of scientific and artistic inquiry in the context of how humanity perceives and interacts with the natural environments we are part of. I am concerned with how scientific and artistic practices generate new meaning and understanding of our place in the world. I enjoy collaborative creative projects and thus far have worked with video, multimedia sculpture, and photography - choosing a media that is aligned with and meaningful to the topic of a given work or project. I have a BSc in Geography, an MA in Applied Art, and a PhD in Glaciology
